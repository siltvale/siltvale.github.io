    Survive the Silt.

    Siltvale is a lore-focused, ARG-inspired Minecraft server built around environmental storytelling, mystery, and immersive roleplay.

    The world is filled with forgotten settlements, abandoned places, hidden records, and clues left behind by those who came before. Rather than simply telling its story, Siltvale allows players to discover it for themselves.

    Explore the world, uncover strange records, solve puzzles and ciphers, and slowly piece together the history of a world that has been lost to time.

    Every ruin, journal, and landmark may hold a piece of something larger.

    Server type

    • Lore-focused
    • ARG-inspired
    • Survival

    Core themes

    • Environmental storytelling
    • Mystery
    • Exploration
    • Roleplay
    • Community

    What we offer

    Immersive Roleplay

    Take part in collaborative storytelling where players help shape the world and its unfolding narrative.

    Environmental Lore

    Discover stories hidden within the environment, from forgotten settlements and landmarks to records left behind by earlier inhabitants.

    Puzzles & Ciphers

    Follow clues, decipher strange messages, and solve ARG-inspired puzzles scattered throughout the world.

    A World to Explore

    Explore a post-apocalyptic world shaped by discovery, player exploration, and the traces of a civilization that came before.

    A Welcoming Community

    Siltvale is built around its players, creating a community-driven experience where people can explore, create, and tell stories together.

    The goal of Siltvale

    To create a world players will remember, and a place they'll always want to return to.

    The History of Siltvale.

    A record of the world before the Plague, the fall of Siltvale, and the generations that eventually returned to reclaim it.

    Historical record

    Age I Before the Silt

    The Age of Discovery

    Eons ago, during what descendants would eventually call the Renaissance period, the world stood on the brink of an age defined by knowledge and machinery.

    The enlightenment shaped the lives of countless peoples. New discoveries were being made, and technology was beginning to move the world toward a future built upon machinery.

    That momentum would never reach its conclusion.

    Age II The Plague

    The Silt

    The Silt Plague spread across the world. Gray and faintly fine beneath the sun, the Silt moved across continents and swallowed the homes of different races.

    It brought tides of disease with it. Biomes withered, settlements disappeared, and the vibrance of the world slowly faded beneath the spreading Silt.

    Natural record / Silt Pale Moss

    Pale moss became one of the remnants associated with the Silt. Life continued to grow within the ruins of the old world, intertwining with abandoned structures and forgotten machinery.

    Age III The Creaking

    The Siltwraiths

    The Silt did not merely consume lives. Over decades, it acquired enough consciousness — enough residue from those it had claimed — to become something new.

    From the drifting haze emerged creatures unlike anything the world had previously known.

    Never turn your back.An old warning passed between survivors
    Don't look away.The Creaking
    Entity record The Creaking

    Siltwraiths became the beginning of the apocalypse. They emerged from the haze as the world was already being consumed, turning the remains of the dead against the living.

    Age IV The Fall

    The End of Siltvale

    The Silt Plague and the apocalypse of the Siltwraiths eventually consumed Siltvale.

    The survivors had little choice. They fled the choking Silt and scattered across distant islands.

    Those scattered populations began forging a thalassocracy — bound together by the ocean and by a shared history that could not be forgotten.

    The homeland was lost, but its history travelled with those who survived.
    Age V Generations later

    The Reclaiming

    Generations passed. The descendants of those populations who survived eventually turned their attention back toward the mainland.

    The Plague has ended and the Siltwraiths have become nocturnal. The mainland — our homeland — is to be reclaimed.

    Word spread across the islands. The homeland that had been abandoned was no longer unreachable.

    The descendants would return to Siltvale and attempt to reclaim what had been lost.

    Age VI The Return

    What Remains

    The Siltwraiths still thrive at night. Their existence raises questions that remain unanswered.

    Question / 001Residue

    Are the Siltwraiths simply residue left behind by death, or is there something more within them?

    Question / 002Behavior

    Do they possess patterns, behaviors, communication, or even emotions?

    Whatever the answer may be, the Siltwraiths remain a reminder of the lives lost during the Plague.

    Age VII The New World

    A Shattered World

    The reclaiming of Siltvale is not simply a battle against monsters. It is a struggle to survive within a world that no longer resembles the one remembered by history.

    Machinery lies abandoned and overgrown, remnants of knowledge from an age that existed before the Plague.

    Descendants rediscover these machines and technologies through knowledge passed down between generations.

    Nature has grown over the remains of civilization. Ruins, machinery, moss, and forgotten technology now exist together.

    Islands foreign to one another, yet bound by the same history, set sail toward the land they once all called home.

    FAQ

    Reader's note

    If you're new to Siltvale, this record should answer most of the questions you might have.

    Before you enter

    Siltvale is a community-driven world. There isn't one story waiting for you to follow. You help create it.

    General questions

    When will Siltvale launch?

    Siltvale is currently in development. An official launch date will be announced once we're confident everything is ready.

    Accepted players will receive the server IP before launch.

    Are there main characters or antagonists?

    No. Siltvale is a community-driven Minecraft server where players contribute to an evolving world through their characters, stories, and interactions.

    It's up to you how you make your story matter.

    Can I stream or make videos?

    Yes! Content creation is encouraged. We love seeing players create videos, streams, screenshots, artwork, and other content inspired by Siltvale.

    Do I need roleplay experience?

    Not at all! Previous roleplay experience is helpful but not required.

    We welcome both experienced and new roleplayers.

    How will we communicate while roleplaying?

    We use both VC and Minecraft chats depending on preference and availability.

    The world

    What's the biome of Siltvale's setting? Is it fully dead?

    Siltvale's settlements have the following biomes: Pale Biome, Dark Forest, Birch Forest, Old Growth Spruce Taiga, and a snowy mountain.

    Therefore, Siltvale is not fully dead, considering it's been eons since the Plague and Apocalypse.

    "We will start where most descendants first arrived at Siltvale."

    Are races fully cosmetic?

    There are exceptions, as long as the race is not used to make the OC overpowered or turn them into a "chosen one" type of character.

    Where will the first RP session begin?

    We will start where most descendants first arrived at Siltvale.

    Groups & civilizations

    Can players create their own groups?

    For groups, what actually matters is the lore and background.

    It depends on the situation. For example, a group could have originally disbanded and your OC decides to recruit people to make it active again.

    Or it could simply be a group your OC decides to establish from scratch.

    Naturally, a recruitment post can then be made for it.

    Will there be player-made civilizations and factions?

    Since the theme is reclamation, the settlements where players can reside are already made.

    Players can add their own builds there. Groups can also create their own bases.

    How do the settlement roles work?

    Settlements already exist as part of Siltvale's reclaimation theme.

    Roles such as Settler, Drifter, Pathfinder and others help define how characters interact with the world.

    Roleplay

    How does roleplay actually work? Is it instant?

    As long as you're around people, yes. However, it's completely okay to be idle if you're by yourself.

    Roleplay naturally happens around the people and situations you're interacting with.

    Can I play multiple roles?

    Yes! You can apply for multiple roles, such as lore writer, builder, moderator, and player at the same time.

    We encourage it as long as you're up for the additional responsibility.

    Will players be able to trade?

    Yes. What players trade for is their choice.

    For example, you could trade one item for another player's item that you need.

    Need more help?

    Who do I contact if I have questions?

    Feel free to open a Ticket or contact a member of the staff team.

    Still have a question?

    Siltvale is still being built. Some answers will change as the world, community, and story continue to develop. If something isn't covered here, ask the community.
